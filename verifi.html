UX/UI Design

Verifi

A mobile news and politics app designed to help users quickly understand how credible a piece of content is — creating a clean, accessible interface so users can scroll, read, and share news with more confidence.

Problem

Information Overload and Unclear Credibility

People constantly see political content across social media and news feeds, but it's difficult to know what is accurate, biased, or misleading. Existing tools often feel cluttered, overly technical, or unapproachable — discouraging everyday users from verifying information before sharing it.

Goals
  • Make credibility information quick to understand at a glance.
  • Keep the interface simple, minimal, and approachable.
  • Improve accessibility with clear labels and contrast-aware visual cues.
My Contribution

My Role & Responsibilities

I collaborated on a team of six designers. My responsibilities included:

  • User Research: Conducted research to understand user trust, credibility needs, and pain points around navigating political information.
  • Color & Visual System: Designed a trustworthy, accessible color palette that avoids bias cues.
  • High-Fidelity Prototyping: Created polished, interactive prototypes for core flows including article views, commenting, and user profiles.
  • Usability Testing: Ran testing sessions, gathered insights, and applied feedback to improve clarity, navigation, and visual hierarchy.
Research

Research Insights

  • Users wanted a single, clear credibility indicator instead of long reports.
  • Overly colorful or biased visual language reduced trust in the tool.
  • Many users were unsure which sources to trust without extra context.
  • Dense layouts and small text made verification tools feel overwhelming.
Solution

A Simple, Trust-Centered Interface

I designed Verifi around a small set of core components that surface credibility in a calm, structured way — focusing on clarity over decoration so users can quickly see how reliable an article is and why.

Credibility

All posts come from verified, credible sources to help users avoid misinformation.

Clean Article Preview

A stripped-down card that prioritizes headline, outlet, date, and credibility over distractions.

Quick Verify Input

A dedicated space to paste a link or search a topic to check credibility quickly.

Take Action

Enables users to sign petitions and participate in advocacy connected to content they care about.

Final Designs

High-Fidelity Mockups

The final UI uses a soft, neutral palette, strong typographic hierarchy, and consistent card components — keeping the experience focused on clarity and trust.

Learnings

Reflection

Designing Verifi taught me how important it is to simplify complex information into digestible UI. I learned to be intentional with color and iconography when designing for trust — to avoid signaling bias or alarm unnecessarily. In future iterations, I would like to run usability tests with different levels of news literacy to refine how credibility is explained, and explore ways to better support users who want to dive deeper into the reasoning behind each rating.
